Privacy Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026

ReactDuel is built so that the answer to "where does my data go" is nowhere. This page explains exactly what the app touches on your device and why — there is no server to send anything to, because ReactDuel doesn't have one.

No data collection No tracking or ads No account required

What stays on your device

Everything ReactDuel creates or remembers is stored locally, using standard iOS app storage (UserDefaults and your device's Application Support folder):

  • Recorded videos — only saved permanently if you explicitly choose to; a session you don't save is deleted, not uploaded anywhere.
  • Play history and streaks — which packs you played and your results, kept so History and streak badges work.
  • Custom packs — any questions or rounds you write yourself stay on your device; they are never shared with other users or with us.
  • Preferences — language, appearance, your chosen accent color, and your watermark handle/logo.

Camera, microphone, and photo access

ReactDuel asks for camera and microphone access to record your reaction, and photo library access only to save a finished video when you tap Save. None of these permissions are used to read anything already on your device — only to write what you create.

Third parties

ReactDuel doesn't use analytics SDKs, ad networks, or crash-reporting services, and makes no network requests of its own. The only way your video ever leaves your device is if you personally share it through iOS's own share sheet — at that point, whatever app or service you chose to share to governs what happens next.

Children's privacy

ReactDuel does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone. There is no account system and no data leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Frequently asked questions

The camera won't open, or shows "Couldn't open camera."

Check Settings → ReactDuel on your device and make sure Camera access is turned on. If you'd rather not grant it, tap "Continue Without Recording" on that screen to play without the camera — your score and history still get saved either way.

Why didn't my video save automatically?

It's intentional — ReactDuel never saves a video without asking first. After a round ends, you'll be asked whether to keep it; choose Save and it's written to your Photos library.

Can I write my own questions?

Yes — every mode has a "Create Pack" option where you can write your own prompts, draft pool, or This-or-That rounds. They're saved on your device and show up alongside the built-in packs.

How do I put my own logo on my videos?

Open Settings → Video Watermark, add your handle and optionally a logo photo, and it'll appear on every video you export from then on.

I found a bug, or have a feature idea.

Email the address above with what you were doing and what happened — screen recordings help a lot if you can include one.
